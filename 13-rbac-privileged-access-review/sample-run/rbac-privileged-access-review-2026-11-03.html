Next steps · 30-day pilot

The agent proposes; people approve. No change is executed automatically.

  1. Risk-free cleanup. Remove the 17 orphaned or redundant assignments through an approved change. Effective access does not change.
  2. Human access to PIM. Convert users' standing privilege into eligibility with JIT activation, MFA and approval.
  3. Non-human identities. Replace Owner/UAA on applications and managed identities with least-privilege roles (Role Designer).
  4. Groups by function and scope. Assign through groups separated by environment and subscription, never to individual users at Management Group level.
  5. Periodic review. Schedule this monthly review in the SRE Agent and track how the indicators evolve.

How to read the Fabric invoice

An F32 capacity is paid for by the running hour (5.76 USD/h). Microsoft breaks that amount down into meters: each workload (Spark, AI, Real-Time…) bills the CUs it used and the rest appears as “Compute Pool”. That is why a high Compute Pool means capacity running unused, not a specific workload.

When the capacity is paused, Fabric bills all pending smoothed operations at once; that is why on some days the total exceeds 32 CUs × running hours. On days with no load the invoice matches the running hours with a maximum error of 0.8%, which validates the running-hours calculation.

How it is calculated

  • Cost is the actual Cost Management amount (ActualCost) for microsoft.fabric/capacities resources, by day and meter, in USD.
  • Running hours come from the resume/suspend events in the Activity Log and are validated against the invoice (ratio invoice ÷ hours × CU).
  • Real work means operations in the Fabric activity log plus Spark sessions; administrative operations and this report's own operations are excluded.
  • Monthly projections scale the observed period to 30 days.

Known limitations

  • The Fabric activity log keeps 27 days: earlier work is not visible.
  • Cost Management publishes cost with a 24–48 h delay; the most recent days may be missing.
  • Hourly averages do not show bursts lasting a few minutes: for sizing and throttling, use Capacity Metrics.
  • When the capacity is paused, Fabric bills all pending smoothed operations at once.

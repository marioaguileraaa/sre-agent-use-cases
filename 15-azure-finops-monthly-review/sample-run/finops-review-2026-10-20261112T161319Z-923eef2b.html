Method

  • Amortized cost: Reservations and savings plans are spread over the days they cover. Currency: USD. Months are calendar months in UTC.
  • Cost Management needs up to 2 days to settle: the forecast and the unusual-day checks use days up to 10 Nov 2026.
  • Forecast = cost of the month so far + the average of the last 7 settled days × the days left. Budget status uses Azure's own budget forecast.
  • Unusual day: more than 25% above the median of the previous 14 days, at least 20 USD higher, and at least 20% above the same weekday a week before.
  • Month-over-month jump: at least 20% and 100 USD more than the previous month. New: cost this month and none in the three months before.
  • Savings are monthly estimates and are not added twice: reservations and savings plans are alternatives, so only the best option counts, and each resource counts once.
  • Allocation: cost of October 2026 by resource, joined with the resource's tags in Azure Resource Graph today.
  • Projects: each resource takes the first project found in its own tag, its resource group's tag (or the tag of the resource that manages the group), its subscription's tag, its cost records' tag and the resource group name rules; the rest is Unallocated. Today's tags apply to every month.

Not covered

  • Reservation and savings plan utilization (needs tenant-level Reservations Reader and Savings plan Reader).
  • Marketplace invoices outside Azure Cost Management, and credits or refunds applied at billing-account level.
  • Usage inside resources (for example idle databases or oversized clusters) beyond what Azure Advisor reports.

# AKS consulting report

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Customer technology leaders, platform owners and Kubernetes operators reviewing
an AKS configuration assessment together. They need an executive reading, an
explainable inventory and evidence-backed actions, without confusing an
unavailable source with a healthy configuration.

## Product Purpose

Present the engine's dated, read-only assessment of AKS, its workloads and
associated resources. Cover best practices, security, FinOps and general
optimization, alongside the five Well-Architected pillars.

## Operating Context

An offline consulting dossier for a brightly lit meeting, a shared presentation
and a printed or PDF report. The same artifact must remain usable on a small
phone and in a matching dark theme. It is not a live monitoring application.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One self-contained HTML file: inline CSS and JavaScript, system fonts, no
  network dependency, framework, analytics or dynamic fetch.
- Render the complete normalized engine model. Do not evaluate Kubernetes
  controls, generate recommendations, infer savings or recalculate scores.
- Accept a legacy single-cluster snapshot or the explicit multi-cluster wrapper.
  Show the supplied aggregate separately from the selected cluster. Failed
  primary reads have no borrowed snapshot, index, topology or cost.
- Use confirmed graph nodes/edges with source, identity and exact-field receipts.
  Keep the conditional Azure reference proposal separate and visibly undeployed.
- Distinguish cluster, namespace, workload and associated-resource observations.
  Filtering, drilldown and exports use all records, not just the current page.
- A local index covers evaluated checks only. Exclude non-applicable checks,
  show unevaluated counts and coverage, and display an unknown score as
  "No evaluado". This is not an official Azure, WARA or Polaris grade.
- Only supplied critical findings enter the critical shortlist. Absence of
  critical findings does not establish absence of risk.
- Cost, utilization and estimated savings need a qualified source and window.
  Missing information is not a zero cost or a promised return.
- Print every matching record, expand disclosures and omit operating controls.
- Preserve resource and source identities in local exports. Shared captures
  use explicitly synthetic or appropriately masked data.
- UI ownership is restricted to this scenario's template, UI tests and design,
  product and surface records. The separate engine owns collection,
  evaluation, catalogs, embedding and runtime instructions; the parent owns
  real data, Azure and deployment.

## Brand Commitments

The user pinned Fluent 2, the Segoe UI system stack, consulting typography,
clean tables, simple score bars and a severity distribution. A subsequent
explicit direction requested a less monochrome appearance and more Microsoft
colors: Microsoft-blue chrome, cover and navigation plus a restrained four-color
brand ribbon. The next explicit direction adds a count-based assessment chart,
prominent but qualified observed cost versus estimated savings, and real Azure
product icons in architecture diagrams. Blue remains the operating accent;
brand colors are not health grades. No gradients, glass, decorative icons,
emoji or generic identical-card dashboard. Semantic Microsoft icons retain
their original licensed SVGs and are not the report's product logo.

Spanish business language for Spain. The public default client is CLIENTE.
Optional authorized client branding is supplied privately as a bounded inline
PNG or JPEG; it is never downloaded by the report. Lab and synthetic provenance
remain explicit and do not imply a production customer cluster.

WARA's Contoso sample output informs the report narrative; Polaris informs
global-to-category-to-namespace-to-workload wayfinding. Neither is installed,
copied as a scoring engine, or represented as the issuer of the local index.

## Evidence on Hand

The confirmed brief supplies the audience, scene, language, brand, structure and
accessibility requirements; no additional aesthetic interview is needed. The
case-03 report is read-only implementation context, not visual authority here.
The engine contracts are legacy schema 1.0 and the explicit
`aks-waf-multi-cluster-v1` wrapper, with a single safe JSON placeholder.
Public UI fixtures are labeled SIMULATED and LAB; they are not live observations.
An opt-in test can read a complete private runtime snapshot by a caller-supplied
local path. That snapshot is not committed, converted to a public fixture or
captured in shareable screenshots.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence and its limitations travel with every conclusion.
2. An unknown is visible and never becomes a healthy zero.
3. Executive reading and technical verification share the same full dataset.
4. Advice remains a human decision, not a remediation or delivery commitment.
5. Offline and private by construction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, native keyboard controls, visible focus, a skip link, semantic
headings, labeled filters and table regions, status text independent of color,
reduced-motion support and printable expanded evidence. Required browser
scenes are 1440px desktop, 390px and 320px mobile, light, dark and print.

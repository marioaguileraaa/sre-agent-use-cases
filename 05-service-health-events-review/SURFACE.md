resource records and the source/coverage ledger, then exports the evidence.

brand exercise or speculative product interview is needed. The report extends
that supplied authority, not the visual identity of cases 00-04.

chronology; coverage-first matrix; resource-first register; category-separated

seeing its resource receipt, unknowns and original text together.

screens stack the rail, filters, list and details with no hidden evidence.

exports retain complete selected records, not a visible page or rounded JS
numbers. No external assets or network activity.
